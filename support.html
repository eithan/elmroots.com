Ratings Now! — common questions

How does Ratings Now! work?

Point your iPhone or iPad camera at a movie or show on your TV, pinch until the title is clear, and tap directly on the title text. The app reads the title and shows its rating and a simple verdict. You can also switch to voice mode and say a title instead.

Are my camera images or voice recordings uploaded?

No. Camera title recognition and voice recognition run on your device. Camera images are not uploaded, and no photos leave your phone. To look up a rating, the app sends only the identified title text to a ratings data provider — never your photo. See our Privacy Policy for details.

A title wasn't recognized. What can I do?

Recognition depends on the title being clearly visible. Pinch to zoom until the title text is large and sharp, tap directly on the words of the title rather than the poster art, and reduce screen glare or reflections. If a title still isn't found, that honest “no match” is by design — the app avoids guessing the wrong title.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Ratings Now! offers a one-time lifetime unlock. On a new device, open the app's settings, choose to unlock unlimited ratings, and select Restore Purchases. As long as you're signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase, your unlock will be restored at no additional charge.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refund requests are handled through Apple rather than Elm Roots. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and select the purchase to request a refund.

Is there an account or subscription?

No. Ratings Now! has no account to create and no subscription. There is a free allowance, an ongoing free daily rating, and an optional one-time lifetime unlock. There are no ads.
