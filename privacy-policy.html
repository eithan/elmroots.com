Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours.

This policy explains how Elm Roots LLC handles information in our applications, including Ratings Now!, and on this website.

Last updated: August 14, 2026

In short: Ratings Now! recognizes titles on your device. Camera images and voice audio are never uploaded and never leave your phone. We do not require an account, we do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

Elm Roots LLC (“Elm Roots,” “we,” “us”) is an independent software company based in Austin, Texas. This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile applications, including Ratings Now!, and to this website at elmroots.com.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in, and we do not collect your name, address, or phone number.
  • We do not upload the images from your camera. Title recognition happens on your device.
  • We do not upload or store voice recordings. Speech is processed on your device.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (such as the IDFA) and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

On-device processing

When you point the camera at a title, Ratings Now! uses your device's built-in text recognition to read the on-screen title. When you use voice mode, your device's built-in speech recognition converts what you say into text. This processing happens on your device. The camera image itself and any audio are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Information used to look up a rating

To return a rating, the app needs to send the identified title — that is, the text of the movie or show, or its catalog identifier — to third-party movie and television data providers. These lookups contain the title only. They do not contain your camera image, your voice audio, your identity, or your location.

Poster images and related details shown in the app are also retrieved from these providers based on the identified title.

Diagnostics and usage data

To keep the app stable and understand which features are used, we collect limited diagnostic and usage information through Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics). This may include:

  • Crash reports and performance data (for example, that a screen failed to load).
  • Aggregate, in-app interaction events (for example, that a scan or voice lookup was attempted).
  • A pseudonymous, app-generated instance identifier and general device and app information (such as device model, operating-system version, and app version).

This information is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. We use it only to operate, maintain, and improve the app.

Purchases

Ratings Now! offers an optional one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited ratings. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple shares limited transaction information with us so we can verify and restore purchases.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of third-party services to provide the app. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) (title data and poster images) — themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
  • OMDb API (supplemental title and rating data) — omdbapi.com
  • Apple (app distribution and in-app purchases) — apple.com/legal/privacy

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share information only with the service providers listed above, solely so they can perform their function for the app, and when required by law.

Data retention

Recent results and your purchase status are stored on your device. Diagnostic and usage data collected through Firebase is retained according to Google's standard retention settings for those services. Because we do not maintain accounts, we do not hold a personal profile of you on our own servers.

Your choices

  • You control the app's access to your camera and microphone at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security.
  • You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app.
  • You can contact us with any question or request regarding your information at the email below.

Children's privacy

Ratings Now! is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our apps evolve or as required by law. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact us at apps@elmroots.com.
